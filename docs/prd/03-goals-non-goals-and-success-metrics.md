## 3. Goals, non-goals and success metrics

The platform succeeds when verified students form project teams and keep collaborating on it, recruiters hire from it on evidence rather than CGPA, and universities and recruiters pay for it.

**Goals**

1. Every account is verified for its role: students and faculty by university email, recruiters by org verification, university admins by claim or invite, staff by staff role (5.27).
2. A student can go from signup to a joined project team and its group chat without leaving the app.
3. Every skill shown on a profile carries an evidence level (L1–L4) that links to its evidence; self-claimed skills are never shown to others (section 5.5).
4. Admins can act on any report within the product (dismiss, remove content, suspend).
5. Ranking inputs are computed from day one, from the first day of the closed beta, so tiers are meaningful at launch.

**Non-goals:** native mobile app, video in posts or chat, video or voice calls, LMS features (courses, grades), paid gig marketplace, likes and follower counts, alumni or mentor accounts, freelance gig payments between students, a data-saver mode.

**Success metrics** (proposed targets for the first 8 weeks after launch; tune after that)

| Metric | Definition | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Onboarding completion | Confirmed signups that reach `/feed` with `onboarding_complete = true` | ≥ 70% |
| GitHub connect rate | Onboarded students with `github_connected = true` | ≥ 40% |
| Team formation | Students in ≥ 1 venture within 14 days of signup | ≥ 30% |
| Join-request response time | Median time from application to accept/decline | ≤ 48 h |
| Weekly active students | Distinct students who post, message or join in a 7-day window | Track; no target until baseline |
| Moderation SLA | Reports resolved (dismissed/removed/suspended) | ≤ 48 h |

Long-range pitch target: 700K users by 2028 (pitch-deck claim, not a product KPI).
