## Scope

Marketing site, Persuade mode: `/` plus `/recruiters`, `/universities`, `/faculty`, `/about`, `/pricing`, `/request-university`. The full plan is in `docs/marketing-design-plan.md`.

## Audience and job

A Pakistani university student on a phone. They should understand within seconds that Skilient turns real project work into proof recruiters trust, then enter their university email and Join. Second audiences (recruiters, universities, faculty) reach their pages from the nav and the "For organisations" rows.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a record you can check: every claim is a real product capture, a sourced figure, or a rule we keep. It refuses the generic SaaS hero with a gradient blob, three equal feature cards, and a logo wall.

OWN-WORLD: Editorial ink and vermillion on warm paper. Spectral display set large and tight, Barlow body, Phosphor regular icons only where they carry meaning. One full-bleed vermillion field ("Our rules"). Teal only on verified proof, tier colours only on the ladder. Real captures sit in plain lg-radius plates or a phone bezel, and two-colour risograph still-lifes appear on the organisation pages.

STORY: Join with your university email. Build with classmates. Get verified by code, teammates and faculty. Rank and CV follow, and recruiters come to you. Proof is free and rank can't be bought.

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 1280×720, the 64 px nav, then an 8/4 split. Left: the two-line Spectral headline (`--text-hero`), the subline at 18 px, and the university email field with Join on one row at the left edge of the copy column. Right: a phone bezel about 340 px wide showing the University Feed capture, cropped hard by the section's bottom edge. At 390×844 it stacks: headline, subline, field and full-width Join, then the top of the phone showing the post being ticked.

FORM: Asymmetric split hero over 14 sections, each a different layout family (gazette list, stat sheet, scroll-snap rail, split, full-width media, ruler, document specimen, bento, manifesto band, index rows, statement plus price, accordion, centred statement, footer). Position 1 of 1: brief-pinned by PRD 5.1 and screen spec 3.1, no concept-seed roll. Signature interaction: the hero focal sequence (tick, then count 11 to 12, then the post moves up one place by FLIP), played once.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
